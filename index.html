01 / ABOUT

I'm a software engineer who enjoys the space where backend systems, data, and real-world problems meet. My work spans enterprise integrations, real-time audit frameworks, and pipelines built to handle more than 100 million records.

At Albertsons Companies India, I work in Merchandising's Pricing subdivision on Competitor Item Insights: bringing fragmented competitive intelligence into a consistent, high-performance view for division leaders and pricing analysts.

Previously at EffiGO Global, I built integration and audit systems, automated document workflows, and improved performance at scale. I'm also exploring how semantic embeddings and applied AI can make product matching more useful.
